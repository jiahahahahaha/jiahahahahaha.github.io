About

I am a researcher working on web security measurement, with a focus on how real-world websites enforce password creation policies. My work builds automated pipelines that discover account registration pages and extract the deployed policy constraints at scale, and studies how those policies vary with site popularity.

I am with the College of Computer Science and Technology, National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), Changsha, China.

Research Interests

  • Password creation policies: minimum/maximum length, required character classes, forbidden inputs
  • Large-scale web measurement and automated page understanding
  • Multi-channel evidence extraction for deployed security configurations
  • LLM-assisted data extraction with deterministic verification
